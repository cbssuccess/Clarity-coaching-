Shining Clarity Coaching

The Thought Record

Catch the thought. Separate fact from story. Name the distortion. Rewrite it without one. Your patterns become visible — and that's where they lose their power.

Everything you write stays on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Step 1

The Thought

Write it exactly how it sounded in your head. Don't clean it up — the raw version is the useful one.

Step 2

Just the Facts

Only what a camera would have recorded. No interpretations, no guesses about what anyone thinks, no predictions. If it's not indisputable, it doesn't go here.

Step 3

Spot the Distortion

Compare the thought to the facts. Where's the gap? Check every distortion doing work in that thought — there's usually more than one.

Step 4

Evidence Against It

What does the distorted thought conveniently ignore? Past evidence, present facts, anything real that argues the other side.

Step 5

The Rewrite

Same situation, minus the distortion. Not forced positivity — just accurate. If the honest version is still uncomfortable, that's allowed. Accurate beats comfortable.

Saved. That's one more pattern on the record instead of running in the dark.

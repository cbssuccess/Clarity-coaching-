Accessibility

Last updated: [date]

Whimsical Dreams wants everyone to be able to browse and shop with us, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification, or other assistive technology.

What we've done

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA. Our product photos have text descriptions, buttons and icons are labeled for screen readers, text colors are chosen to be easy to read, and the whole site can be used with a keyboard.

Need help? We're happy to assist.

If anything on this site is hard to use, or you'd like help choosing or ordering an item, text us at 661-703-5900 or message us on Facebook. We'll describe items, answer questions, and take your order directly.

Tell us about a problem

We're a small shop and we're always improving. If you find a part of the site that doesn't work for you, please let us know and we'll fix it as quickly as we can.

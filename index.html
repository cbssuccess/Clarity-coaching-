01 / About

Hi, I'm Cait.

I design, build and maintain websites for small businesses, and I'm also a life coach helping people reach their personal goals and build confidence. I create workbooks, planners, frameworks and other tools to make life a little lighter and brighter for others. I'm picky about the details, I love working with AI tools, and I'm always learning.

Learn more ↓Show less ↑

I co-founded Shining Clarity Coaching, where I help people untangle stuck thinking patterns and change beliefs that hold them back. Along the way I've written coaching frameworks, workbooks and planners, and I've built the websites and interactive tools that go with them. I also build websites for small businesses that don't have one yet.

I work with AI daily, and I don't take its word for things. I check its answers, question what sounds too confident and push for honest feedback over easy agreement. I'm also taking a course in vibe coding, because I want to understand how these tools actually work.

Underneath it all, I care about creating things that actually work. I'm not interested in making filler. Whether it's a website, a workbook or a planner, it has to be useful to the person on the other end. That's why I care so much about clear writing, good judgment and tone. I can usually tell when something is correct but unhelpful, or polished but wrong.

I can't solve a problem for someone, but I can hand them tools that make their life a little easier, brighter or lighter. The rest is up to them.

How a website project works

  1. We talk. I learn about your business and what customers ask.
  2. I build a sample. You see a real, working draft before you decide anything.
  3. We polish it. You tell me what to change until it feels right.
  4. You own your domain. It's registered in your name from day one, and I can host the site for you if you'd like.

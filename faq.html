Frequently Asked Questions

What is coaching and how is it different from therapy?
Coaching is forward focused. We don't dig into your past — we work with where you are now and where you want to go. We're not treating a condition or processing trauma; we're identifying patterns and creating shifts.
I've tried coaching before and it didn't work. Why would this be different?
Because we don't start with generic advice. You get both of us from day one — two trained perspectives on your specific pattern, not one-size-fits-all coaching.
Do I need to have a specific problem or goal to start?
No. Most people come in knowing something is off but not being able to name exactly what. That's fine — figuring out what the loop actually is, is part of the work.
How do I know which service is right for me?
Start with a free discovery call — that's exactly what it's for. We'll talk through what's going on and recommend the right fit based on what you need.
How do I get started?
Fill out the form on our Apply page. We'll be in touch within 48 hours to schedule your free discovery call.
Why are spots limited?
Because this work only works when there's enough space to do it well. We keep client spots limited so every person gets the full attention they deserve.
Do you offer packages?
Yes — our Loop Breaker Method and Identity Rewire Experience are our core packages. Both include both coaches.
What is your refund policy?
We offer a full refund if you cancel before your first session. If you need to cancel within 48 hours of a session, we'll reschedule instead of charging.
What are your business hours?
We work with clients across time zones. Availability varies — we'll share current open times when you apply.

Still have questions? Reach out — we're happy to help.

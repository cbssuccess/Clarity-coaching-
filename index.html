03 / About

Hi, I'm Cait.

What I do

I build clean, simple websites for small businesses, make planners and workbooks for people whose lives are full, and make art.

Why I do it

A good website shouldn't be complicated or out of reach for a small local business, and hard things feel more doable with the right tool. I like taking something complicated and turning it into something clear and usable, like a planner that fits how a busy brain really works, or a workbook built on one idea: your worth is given, not earned.

I'm a writer, web designer, and maker in Twin Bridges, Montana.

"Caitra" is a nickname my mom made by blending my name with my sister's. It's stuck with me, and it's why this is Caitra's Creations.

How a website project works

  1. We talk. I learn about your business and what customers ask.
  2. I build a sample. You see a real, working draft before you decide anything.
  3. We polish it. You tell me what to change until it feels right.
  4. You own your domain. It's registered in your name from day one, and I can host the site for you if you'd like.

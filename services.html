What We Offer

Our Services

Every offering is designed to help you get out of the loop, find the belief underneath the stuckness, and move with clarity.

Focused Session
90 minutes · $197

You bring one specific situation you can't get clarity on — a decision, a reaction you keep having, or a pattern that shows up occasionally. We look at it with you from two angles and show you the belief driving your response. You leave with a clear explanation of what's causing the stuck point and one practical next step that moves the situation forward.

This is for you if:

  • You're stuck on one issue
  • You want to understand what's behind it
  • You want a clear next step, not ongoing support

What you can expect:

  • A straightforward explanation of what's driving the problem
  • Why you keep reacting the same way
  • One action that will actually move things forward

What this is NOT:

  • Therapy
  • Crisis support
  • A long-term container

Result: You stop looping on the same problem because you finally understand what's behind it and what to do next.

Loop Breaker Method
6–8 weeks · $1,500

You already know your pattern — you can see it happening, but you can't stop it. Over several weeks, we track it with you in real time. One of us watches how it shows up in your story; the other listens for the belief underneath it. You get weekly sessions plus structured check-ins so you can interrupt the pattern while it's happening, not after.

This is for you if:

  • You keep repeating the same behavior even when you know better
  • You want help changing your response in real time
  • You want support between sessions, not just insight

What you can expect:

  • Weekly sessions
  • Two Voxer check-ins per week to help you respond differently
  • Clear explanations of what's driving the pattern
  • Guidance on interrupting it as it shows up

What this is NOT:

  • A one-time clarity session
  • A place to vent
  • Identity-level work

Result: You stop ending up in the same place because you learn to interrupt the pattern as it's happening.

Identity Rewire Experience
10–12 weeks · $3,500

This is for situations where the issue isn't a single pattern — it's the way you see yourself. Across 10–12 weeks, we work with you to identify the core belief that sits underneath most of your decisions, reactions, and default responses. You get weekly sessions, daily Voxer access, and workbook support to help you apply the work in real situations. The goal is to change the internal setting that keeps creating the same outcomes, even when you change your behavior.

This is for you if:

  • You've changed habits, jobs, relationships, or environments but the same internal issue keeps showing up
  • You can feel the problem is deeper than a pattern — it's how you think about yourself
  • You want daily support as you practice new ways of responding

What you can expect:

  • Weekly deep-dive sessions
  • Daily Voxer access within a designated response window
  • Workbook tools to reinforce the new belief between sessions
  • Two people tracking blind spots you can't see on your own

What this is NOT:

  • A quick fix
  • A place to vent
  • Therapy or emotional processing work
  • A surface-level mindset program

Result: Your default self-story changes. The belief that used to limit your decisions stops being the one in charge, and your reactions, confidence, and choices shift because the foundation underneath them has changed.

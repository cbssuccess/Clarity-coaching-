Whimsical Dreams respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

This website has no accounts, no checkout, and no sign-up forms. When you order by text or Facebook, we receive the information you send us — such as your name, phone number, and, for shipped orders, your mailing address.

How we use it

We use your information only to fill and deliver your order and to reply to your messages. We don't sell, rent, or share your information, except with the shipping carrier as needed to deliver your package.

This website

This site doesn't use cookies or tracking/analytics tools. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers when you visit; see Google's privacy policy for how they handle that. Links to Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok are covered by those companies' own policies.
